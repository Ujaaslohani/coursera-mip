The Problem, In One Picture

Right now, the evidence is scattered

A single course module produces evidence in six different places at once. Today those six places don't talk to each other — so a content team only ever sees a slice of the real story.

🎬
Video lecture
Reviewed on its own, by whoever watches it
↔↕
🖼️
Slides & diagrams
Reviewed separately by content design
↔↕
✅
Quiz attempts
Reviewed as pass/fail stats, alone
↔↕
💬
Discussion forum
Skimmed by moderators, rarely cross-checked

Each modality is reviewed in isolation — nobody connects the dots across all of them at once.

The platform's job is to pull all six modalities into one governed pipeline, so a single question — "why are learners struggling here?" — gets answered with evidence from every modality at once, cited and ready for a human to approve.

Not One AI Call — A Team of Them

Reference

Terms worth knowing

A short glossary for anyone new to the platform's vocabulary.

Asset
Any registered piece of content — a video, slide, transcript, quiz, or discussion thread — with an owner, topic, and permission scope.
Segment
A small, timestamped or location-tagged piece of an asset — e.g. one video moment, one slide, one forum post — small enough to compare across modalities.
Meaning fingerprint (embedding)
A numeric representation of a segment's meaning, which lets conceptually similar content — even across formats — be found by search.
Cross-modal retrieval
Searching video, image, text, quiz, and discussion evidence together, in response to a single plain-language question.
Grounded synthesis
An AI-generated explanation that only makes claims traceable to specific, cited evidence — nothing invented.
Insight pack
The structured output of synthesis: a finding, a recommendation, and the evidence behind both, ready for human review.
Human review
The required approval step where a person accepts, edits, or rejects an insight pack before it becomes an operational action.
Feedback loop
Reviewer decisions flowing back into the system, improving future retrieval ranking and synthesis quality over time.
